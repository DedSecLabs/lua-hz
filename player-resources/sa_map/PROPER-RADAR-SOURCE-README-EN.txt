------- Instructions:


= How to use:
Open the .max with 3DS Max (it was saved with 2020).
Download the "radar-sat-8k.png" (shortcut in the download), which is the high resolution satellite image of the game (built by Jéssica Natália).
Use Scene Explorer to hide the radar background and overlay.
Use only the "Top" camera to move.
Use Slate Material Editor for a simple overview of colors.


= How to render:
Change the scene view camera to "Top render camera". DO NOT MOVE THIS CAMERA! It needs to be at "0.0 0.0 -1000.0".
Open Render Setup.
Set the resolution in "Width" and "Height". I recommend "8192" as a base, as you will later use it in SA:DE.
Click "Render" and wait.
Use the "Save Image" button to save in .png. The water around is the transparency channel.
Open it in an image editor such as Photoshop.
Use the dropper to pick up the color of the small water (eg in Las Venturas) and paint the background.
Save the image in .png at the proper resolution for the radar (eg 6144 for 512px tiles, use 8192 only for the SADE texture). IMPORTANT: USE "BILINEAR" RESAMPLING!!!
Finally, for compiling in .txd for classic GTA, use GTA Radar Creator: https://forum.mixmods.com.br/f307-utilities/t6093-iii-vc-sa-lcs-vcs-gta-radar-creator-merge-split

HQ Weapon Icons for San Andreas — Zera

Original author: Zera
Original release: https://www.gtagarage.com/mods/show.php?id=21062

LuaHZ uses 46 unmodified 256x256 PNGs from Zera's original icon pack.
LuaHZ renames the files to its weapon-ID resource names; the original-to-ID
mapping and exact hashes are recorded in this directory's SOURCE.json in
the source repository. Zera's artwork is separate from LuaHZ's MIT code.

Zera's original readmes permit use in another mod with author credit and
restrict rehosting the standalone mod without permission. The original
English and Portuguese notices accompany these files as ZERA-README-EN.txt
and ZERA-README-PT.txt. This is inclusion in LuaHZ under that mod-use grant,
not a standalone mirror or a claim that LuaHZ created these icons.
